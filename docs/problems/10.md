<div class="problem">
A glass suncatcher is in the shape of an equilateral triangle with sides of length $144\text{mm}$. The triangle is labeled $\triangle \rm ABC$ and divided 
into $8$ smaller sections as follows.
<ul>
    <li>Sides $\rm AB$ and $\rm BC$ are each divided into $8$ segments of equal length.</li>
    <li>Each point of division on $\rm AB$ is connected to its corresponding point of division on $\rm BC$, creating $7$ line segments.</li>
    <li>Each of the $7$ line segments is parallel to the third side of the triangle, $\rm AC$.</li>
</ul>

One of the sections is coloured blue, as shown. An altitude is constructed from $\rm A$ to $\rm D$ on $\rm BC$, dividing the blue section into two parts.

[{
ctx.scale = 225

A = (.5, (3**.5) / 2)
B = (0, 0)
C = (1, 0)
D = (.5, 0)

Xs = [(C[0]/8 * i, 0) for i in range(1, 8)]
Ys = [(A[0]/8 * i, A[1]/8 * i) for i in range(1, 8)]

ctx.add(Polygon(A, B, C))

for n, (Xi, Yi) in enumerate(zip(Xs, Ys)):
    if n in {4, 5}:
        continue
    ctx.add(Path(Xi, Yi))

ctx.add(Polygon(Xs[4], Xs[5], Ys[5], Ys[4]), Fill(LIGHT_BLUE))
ctx.add(Path(A, D))

ctx.add(Label(.5, A[1]+.05, r"\rm A"))
ctx.add(Label(-.05, -.05, r"\rm B"))
ctx.add(Label(1.05, -.05, r"\rm C"))
ctx.add(Label(.5, -.05, r"\rm D"))
}]

In the blue section, determine the ratio of the area on the left side of the altitude to the area on the right side of the altitude.
</div>

_Proposed as the [problem of the week #25 of year 2025/26](https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/sites/default/files/documents/2026/POTWE-25-G-25-P.html), by the Centre for Education in Mathematics and Computing of the University of Waterloo._

<div class="solution">
Let ${\rm X}_i$ be the $i$th point of division of the side $\rm BC$, let ${\rm Y}_i$ be that of $\rm AB$, and let ${\rm Z}_i$ be the $i$th intersection &ndash;
starting from the bottom &ndash; of $\rm AD$ with ${\rm X}_i {\rm Y}_i$; we thus want to find $[\rm Y_5 Y_6 Z_2 Z_1] : [\rm X_5 X_6 Z_2 Z_1]$.

[{
ctx.scale = 225

A = (.5, (3**.5) / 2)
B = (0, 0)
C = (1, 0)
D = (.5, 0)

Xs = [(C[0]/8 * i, 0) for i in range(1, 8)]
Ys = [(A[0]/8 * i, A[1]/8 * i) for i in range(1, 8)]
Zs = [(.5, A[1]/4 * i) for i in range(1, 4)]

ctx.add(Polygon(A, B, C))

ctx.add(Polygon(Xs[4], Xs[5], Zs[1], Zs[0]), Fill(LIGHT_BLUE))
ctx.add(Polygon(Xs[3], Xs[4], Zs[0]), Fill(BLUE))
ctx.add(Polygon(Ys[4], Ys[5], Zs[1], Zs[0]), Fill(LIGHT_GREEN))
ctx.add(Polygon(Ys[5], A, Zs[1]), Fill(GREEN))

for n, (Xi, Yi) in enumerate(zip(Xs, Ys)):
    if n not in {4, 5}:
        ctx.add(Path(Xi, Yi))
    if n != 3:
        ctx.add(Label(Xi[0], Xi[1] - .05, rf"\rm X_{n+1}"))
    ctx.add(Label(Yi[0] - .05, Yi[1] + .05, rf"\rm Y_{n+1}"))

ctx.add(Path(A, D))

ctx.add(Label(.5, A[1]+.05, r"\rm A"))
ctx.add(Label(-.05, -.05, r"\rm B"))
ctx.add(Label(1.05, -.05, r"\rm C"))
ctx.add(Label(.5, -.05, r"\rm D"))
}]

Notice that all of the triangles of the form $\triangle {\rm D} {\rm X}_j {\rm Z}_{j-4}$ (for $j > 4$) are similar due to Thales' theorem and point number 3 
in the statement, and in particular $\rm \triangle D X_6 Z_2 \sim \triangle D C A$; hence, because $\rm DX_6 = DC/2$,
$$
\rm [\triangle D X_6 Z_2] = \frac 1 4 [\triangle D C A] = \frac 1 8 DC \ DA = \frac 1 8 72 \cdot 144 \sin 60^\circ = 648\sqrt 3 \text{mm}^2.
$$
By an analogous reasoning we can compute $[\rm D X_5 Z_1]$ and find $[\rm X_5 X_6 Z_2 Z_1]$ as the difference between the two:
$$
\rm [\triangle D X_6 Z_2] = \frac 1 {16} [\triangle D C A] = 162\sqrt 3 \text{mm}^2
    \implies \color{green} \boxed{[\rm X_5 X_6 Z_2 Z_1] = 486\sqrt 3 \text{mm}^2.}
$$
<br>

To obtain $[\rm Y_5 Y_6 Z_2 Z_1]$, we now look at triangles of the form $\triangle {\rm A} {\rm Y}_k {\rm Z}_{k-4}$ &ndash; which are again similar one 
another by the same arguments discussed above &ndash; and realize that it again can be obtained as the subtraction of two of them, namely
$\triangle \rm A Y_5 Z_1$ and $\triangle \rm A Y_6 Z_2$. Therefore:
$$
\begin{align*}
\rm [\triangle A Y_5 Z_1] &= \frac 9 {16} [\triangle A Y_4 D] = \frac 9 {32} Y_4 Z_2\ DA = 729 \sqrt{3} \text{mm}^2,\\
\rm [\triangle A Y_6 Z_2] &= \frac 1 4 [\triangle A Y_4 D] = 324 \sqrt{3} \text{mm}^2;
\end{align*}
$$
and putting everything together:
$$
[\rm Y_5 Y_6 Z_2 Z_1] = [\triangle A Y_5 Z_1] - [\triangle A Y_6 Z_2] = \color{green} \boxed{405 \sqrt 3 \text{mm}^2.}
$$
<br>

Finally, the requested ratio turns out to be $405/486 = 5/6$.
</div>
